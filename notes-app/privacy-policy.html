Privacy Policy

Notes (iOS & Android) · Effective date: September 7, 2026

This policy explains what data the Notes app ("the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It covers both the Android and iOS ("NotesFlow") versions of the app; sections below note where the two platforms differ. Notes is developed and maintained by an individual developer. If you have questions, contact therahulpahuja@gmail.com.

1. Your notes stay on your device

Note content — titles, text, tags, categories, images, drawings, file attachments, and voice recordings you create — is stored locally on your device in the app's own database. We do not upload your note content to our servers, and we do not sell your data to anyone. On iOS, note titles and content are additionally encrypted at rest using a key stored in the device's Keychain. This key is included in encrypted backups and travels with your data if you move to a new device, so your notes remain readable after a backup restore or device upgrade. In the rare case a note can't be decrypted on a given device, the app tells you clearly rather than showing unreadable text.

Note content only leaves your device in the following cases, all of which you control:

  • Optional cloud AI features (Android only). If you enable Magic Sort (cloud AI) or AI meeting summaries in Settings, the relevant note or meeting transcript text is sent to Google's Gemini API for processing and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. These features are off unless you turn them on; when off, sorting and summaries run entirely on-device. On iOS, there is no cloud AI option at all — semantic search ("Talk to Notes"), summarizing, organizing, converting to a checklist, duplicate detection, and translation all run entirely on-device using Apple's frameworks (Apple Intelligence where available, NaturalLanguage, Translation, Vision). Your note content never leaves an iOS device for any AI feature.
  • Importing from Google Keep. If you use the "Import Keep" feature, notes are read from your Google Keep account only when you initiate the import, and are not retained or transmitted beyond creating the local note.
  • Feature requests and issue reports (anonymous). If you use "Request a Feature" or "Raise an Issue" in Settings, the message you type or dictate is sent to our Firebase Realtime Database so we can review it. This is anonymous — no account ID, email, or device identifier is attached, regardless of whether you're signed in.
  • Error diagnostics. When the app encounters a handled error, a short diagnostic record (which part of the app, a brief error message — never your note content) is sent to our Firebase Realtime Database and logged as an analytics event, purely to help us find and fix bugs. This is anonymous in the same way feedback submissions are.

2. Account information

Android: Signing in uses Google Sign-In (via Firebase Authentication). We receive your Google account's name, email address, and profile photo to identify your account within the app. We do not receive your Google password.

iOS: Sign in with Apple or Google is optional, and is currently disabled in the shipped app. When enabled, it only prefills a local profile (name, email) shown within the app — that information is stored on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. Signing out deletes the locally saved profile.

4. Device permissions

Android

  • Microphone — to record voice notes, meeting audio, and power live speech-to-text.
  • Camera — to attach photos or scans to a note.
  • Notifications — to show reminder and update notifications.
  • Exact alarms — to trigger reminders at the precise time you set.

Speech-to-text is performed using Android's built-in speech recognition service on your device or through your device's default assistant/TTS service — audio is not sent to our servers for this purpose.

iOS

  • Microphone & Speech Recognition — to record voice memos and meeting audio, and to transcribe speech to text.
  • Camera — to scan documents or record a video note.
  • Photo Library — only when you attach, scan, or save an image you pick.
  • Face ID — to unlock notes you've password- or biometric-protected.
  • Notifications — to show reminder and save-confirmation alerts.

Speech-to-text uses Apple's on-device speech recognition when your selected language supports it. For a language without on-device support, Apple's speech-recognition servers process the audio instead, subject to Apple's Privacy Policy — audio is never sent to our own servers either way. Reminder and save-confirmation notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data described above is shared only with the service providers necessary to operate the app — Google/Firebase (authentication on Android, analytics, crash reporting, push notification registration, remote config, database), Amplitude (product analytics; see Amplitude's Privacy Policy), Apple (on-device intelligence and, for some languages, server-based speech recognition, on iOS), and, only when you opt in on Android, Google's Generative AI API. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.

6. Data retention and deletion

Notes remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. To request deletion of account-linked data we hold (such as feedback submitted while signed in on Android, or your push notification token), email therahulpahuja@gmail.com from the account in question and we will remove it. Feedback, feature requests, and error reports submitted from iOS are anonymous and aren't linked to any account we could look them up by.

7. Children's privacy

Notes is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Security

We rely on our processors' security infrastructure — Firebase and Amplitude (encryption in transit, access-controlled cloud storage) — to protect the data described above. On iOS, note titles and content are additionally encrypted at rest using a key stored in the device's Keychain, which travels with encrypted backups and device migrations so your notes remain readable afterward. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy is subject to change as the app evolves. We may update it from time to time without individual notice, so please check back periodically — material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
